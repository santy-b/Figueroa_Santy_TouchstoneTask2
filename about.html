About North Star Bakery

Our Story

Founded in 2018, North Star Bakery began as a small home kitchen project and grew into a neighborhood staple. We believe in bringing people together through wholesome, delicious baked goods.

Sourcing Philosophy

We source 100% of our flour from organic local grain mills and partner with regional dairy farms to ensure our ingredients are fresh, ethical, and sustainable.
